## Step-by-Step: Adding Stockflow Assets to Canva

1. Download your chosen asset from [Stockflow.media](https://stockflow.media/) — MP4 or JPEG
2. Open your Canva project
3. Click **Uploads** > **Upload files** > select your downloaded file
4. Drag the asset onto your canvas
5. Resize, reposition, and add text or other elements on top

> **Tip:** For video backgrounds, upload the MP4 and set it as the page background. For image posts, use JPEG files — up to 8K for maximum sharpness.
