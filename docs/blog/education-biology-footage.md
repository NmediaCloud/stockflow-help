## Introduction

Teaching biology, microbiology, or life sciences requires visual authenticity — and nothing communicates that better than real microscopic footage. Stockflow.media provides educators with professional, royalty-free microscopic visuals for classroom, e-learning, and lecture use.

---

## Educational Use Cases

### University Lectures
Use 16:9 MP4 clips or JPEG slides in:
- PowerPoint and Google Slides lecture decks
- Recorded lecture videos (Zoom, Teams, Loom)
- Lab manual image supplements

### Online Courses
Embed microscopic B-roll in:
- Course introduction and chapter transition videos
- Visualisation segments to illustrate abstract concepts
- Quiz image questions with real specimen imagery

### High School Biology Classes
- Print A1 classroom posters using 8K JPEG images
- Show MP4 clips on projectors during lessons
- Use visuals in science fair presentations

### E-Learning Modules
Drop images and video clips into:
- Articulate Storyline / Rise 360
- Adobe Captivate
- iSpring, Lectora, or any SCORM-compatible platform
