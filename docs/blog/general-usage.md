# Stockflow.media Asset Guide: Creative Use Cases

Stockflow.media is a premium stock media library designed for professionals who need **high-resolution, royalty-free assets** that look exceptional across every medium — from social media to cinema screens, from restaurant menus to museum exhibitions.

This guide gives you a broad overview of where and how our versatile collections are most effectively used.

---

## Who Uses Stockflow Assets?

| Creator Type | Common Use Case |
|---|---|
| **Filmmakers & Documentarians** | B-roll footage, cinematic overlays, science visualisations |
| **Restaurant & Hospitality Brands** | Menu photography, social media marketing, decor prints |
| **Digital Marketers & Social Teams** | Reels, YouTube thumbnails, sponsored content |
| **Science Communicators & Educators** | Infographic backgrounds, lecture slide visuals, exhibit art |
| **Print Designers & Publishers** | Magazine spreads, large-format prints, coffee table books |
| **Web & UI Designers** | Hero images, animated backgrounds, icon overlays |

---

## Matching Assets to Your Project

### By Format
- **MP4 Video** → Documentaries, social media Reels and Shorts, presentations
- **8K JPEG/TIFF Images** → Print design, editorial photography, digital backgrounds
- **Vector Files** → Branding, infographics, scalable illustrations, web design
- **MP3 Audio** → Video soundscapes, podcast intros, ambient backgrounds

### By Collection Theme
- **Microscopic** → Science, health, biology, and technology narratives
- **Food & Beverage** → Culinary, restaurant, hospitality, and lifestyle content
