## How It Works in Your Recording Setup

### Option 1: OBS Studio (Free, Windows/Mac)
1. Download a **16:9 MP4 loop** from Stockflow.media
2. In OBS, add a **Media Source** → select your MP4 file → enable **Loop**
3. Layer your webcam feed on top (use chroma key or portrait segmentation)
4. Go live or record — your background plays as a seamless loop

### Option 2: Zoom / Teams Virtual Background
1. Download an **8K JPEG** for a static background, or **MP4** for animated
2. In Zoom: Settings → Background & Filters → Add your file
3. Works with or without a green screen

### Option 3: Post-Production (Premiere / DaVinci)
1. Place your recorded talking-head footage on the timeline
2. Add Stockflow MP4 as a background layer behind your video
3. Use masking or chroma removal to blend your subject into the scene
