## Use Cases in Academic Contexts

### Conference Posters (A0 / A1 Print)
- Use **8K JPEG images** as full-bleed poster backgrounds
- Standard A0 poster (841×1189mm at 300 DPI) requires ~10,000px — our 8K images comfortably exceed this
- Example: A histopathology slide image as the visual anchor for a cancer research poster

### PowerPoint / Keynote Presentations
- Use images as **slide backgrounds** or **content images**
- Use **16:9 MP4 loops** as animated backgrounds in opening/closing slides
- Keep animations subtle — science audiences expect visual clarity over decoration

### Academic Journal Supplementary Materials
- Use JPEG images as **figure illustrations** to accompany written descriptions
- Example: "Figure 1: Diatom shell structure (Stockflow.media, royalty-free)"

### University Lecture Slides
- Drop MP4 clips into lecture videos as B-roll for recorded modules
- Use images to illustrate abstract concepts (cell division, parasite life cycle, mycelium networks)

### Science Fair and Exhibition Displays
- Print large-format JPEG images onto foam boards, canvas, or vinyl banners
- Use as exhibition display backdrops or specimen illustration panels
