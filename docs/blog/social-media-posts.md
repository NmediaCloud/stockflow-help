## Tips for Maximising Social Media Reach

1. **Use video over static** whenever possible — all major platforms currently favour Reels/Shorts in algorithmic reach.
2. **Add captions and text overlays** to your videos. 85% of social videos are watched on mute.
3. **Batch-create content** — download a full subcategory (e.g., "Biriyani") and schedule 5–10 posts from one collection.
4. Pair our footage with on-trend **royalty-free audio** from our Audio collection for a fully licensed post.
