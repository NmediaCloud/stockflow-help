## Introduction

Short-form video dominates content consumption. Science content — particularly microscopic visuals and food cinematics — performs exceptionally well on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts because it is visually unexpected and curiosity-triggering.

## High-Performing Content Formats

### Science Fact Videos
**Formula:** Microscopic visual background + bold text overlay + voiceover

- Example: *Diatom shell clip + "These single-celled organisms produce 20% of Earth's oxygen"*
- Collections: [Diatoms](../collections/diatoms.md), [Algae Bloom](../collections/algae-bloom.md)

### Mystery Reveal Format
**Formula:** Close-up clip — zoom out text reveal

- Example: *"What is this? — It's E. Coli dividing under a microscope"*
- Collections: [E. Coli](../collections/e-coli.md), [Bacteria](../subcategories/bacteria.md)

### Food Satisfying Videos
**Formula:** Beautiful slow-motion food clip with minimal caption

- Example: *Biriyani or noodle steam clip with soft background music*
- Collections: [Biriyani](../collections/biriyani.md), [Noodles](../collections/noodles.md)

### Countdown Format
**Formula:** 5 short microscopic clips stitched together with numbered text

- Collections: [Bacteria](../subcategories/bacteria.md), [Parasite](../subcategories/parasite.md), [Fungi](../subcategories/fungi.md)

## Creating a Short-Form Video in 5 Minutes

1. Download a 9:16 MP4 from [Stockflow.media](https://stockflow.media/)
2. Open CapCut, InShot, or Adobe Premiere Rush
3. Import the clip and trim to 15–30 seconds
4. Add text overlay with your fact or hook
5. Add music from a royalty-free library
6. Export and upload directly to TikTok, Reels, or Shorts
