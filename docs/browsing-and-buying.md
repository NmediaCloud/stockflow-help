# Browsing & Buying

Stockflow.media is built around a fast, visual **gallery** — browse thousands of
previews, open any asset on its own page, and license the full-resolution file in
a couple of clicks. No subscription, pay once, download instantly.

## 1. Browse the gallery

Head to the [Stockflow.media gallery](https://stockflow.media/gallery/) and explore:

- **By category** — Food & Beverage, Microscopic, Catering Events, Background, and Retail Items, each broken into subcategories and themed collections.
- **By search** — type what you need ("coffee latte", "ocean ripples", "cancer cell") and filter by type (image / video) and aspect ratio.

Every thumbnail links straight to that asset's own page.

## 2. Open an asset page

Each asset has a dedicated landing page showing a **large watermarked preview**,
its title, resolution, format, and price. This page *is* the licensing page —
there's no separate "product" step.

## 3. License it

Click **License this asset** (or the price button). The purchase panel opens
right there on the page — no redirect, you stay where you are.

## 4. Sign in & add wallet credit

- **Sign in** (or create an account) so your purchases and downloads are saved.
- Top up your **wallet** once — then licensing any asset is a single click, with
  the price deducted from your balance. No re-entering payment each time.

## 5. Download instantly

Confirm the purchase and the **full-resolution, watermark-free original**
downloads immediately. It's yours to use across all your projects, forever, under
our [royalty-free license](license.md).

Your purchases stay in your account — revisit **My Purchases** anytime to
re-download.
