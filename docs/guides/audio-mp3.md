# How to Use Audio (MP3) Assets from Stockflow.media

Stockflow.media provides high-quality, royalty-free audio assets in **MP3 format**, designed to complement our visual collections across documentaries, social media, presentations, and creative projects.

---

## Audio Asset Types

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **Ambient Soundscapes** | Natural, environmental backgrounds | Documentaries, nature films |
| **Music Stems** | Individual instrument layers | Custom mixing and scoring |
| **Cinematic Tracks** | Full orchestrated scores | Trailers, intros, feature films |

---

## Importing MP3 Files Into Your Project

### Adobe Audition / Premiere Pro
1. Import the `.mp3` file via **File > Import**.
2. Drag it to the **Audio Track** on your timeline.
3. Use the **Essential Sound** panel to set it as Ambient, Music, or SFX.

### GarageBand (Mac)
1. Drag the `.mp3` directly into a GarageBand track.
2. Use the **Smart Controls** panel to adjust EQ and reverb.

### DaVinci Resolve (Fairlight)
1. Open the **Fairlight** page.
2. Import via the **Media Pool** and place on the timeline.
3. Use bus routing for multi-track blending.

---

## Tips for Best Results

- Layer **ambient audio** underneath narration to add realism without overpowering the voice.
- Use music stems to remove or highlight specific instruments when your visuals need more or less energy.
- For social content, keep audio under **-6 dBFS** to avoid clipping on mobile devices.
- All MP3 assets are **royalty-free** once licensed.
