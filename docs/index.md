## 1. Welcome to Stockflow
Stockflow.media is a curated marketplace designed by artists for creators, social media managers, and filmmakers. We offer professional-grade 4K food and beverage assets in specific formats (16:9, 9:16, and 1:1) for a fraction of the cost of traditional stock sites.

**The Best Part:** No monthly subscriptions. Buy only what you need, when you need it.

## 2. Navigating the Marketplace
Our interface is designed to be "Goldilocks" simple—not too much, not too little.

### The Header & Search
- **Search Bar:** Located at the top, you can search by title (e.g., "Pouring Coffee") or by tags (e.g., "Macro," "Slow Motion").
- **Help Icon (ⓘ):** Click the "i" icon at any time to re-watch the video walkthrough or access this documentation.
- **My Purchases (📜):** Once signed in, this is your personal vault where you can re-download any asset you’ve previously licensed.

### Category Filters
- **Super Categories:** Toggle between "Featured" assets and specific niches like "Food & Beverage."
- **Subcategories:** Drill down into specific types, such as "Cafe," "Bar," or "Kitchen."
- **Format Selector:** Instantly filter for Vertical (9:16) for TikTok/Reels, Wide (16:9) for YouTube, or Square (1:1) for Instagram posts.

## 3. How to Purchase & Download
We use a **Wallet-Based System** to make checkouts instant and secure.
1. **Sign In:** Use your Google account for a one-click login.
2. **Add Funds:** Click "Add Funds" in the header. We offer top-up tiers powered by Stripe for 100% secure transactions.
3. **Preview:** Click any video card to open the High-Def Preview Modal. You can see the full resolution, tags, and license price here.
4. **License & Download:** Click "Download." The amount will be deducted from your wallet, and you will receive:
   - An instant high-res download link.
   - A confirmation email with the link.
   - A permanent copy in your "My Purchases" tab.

## 5. Understanding Your License
Our license is built for modern creators.
- **Permitted:** Social media (TikTok, Reels, YouTube), Commercial Ads, Website/App backgrounds, and Client projects.
- **One Project Rule:** Each purchase licenses the clip for use in one project/campaign. To use the same clip for a different client, simply re-purchase.
- **Ownership:** Nmediaservices retains the copyright; you are purchasing a perpetual license to use the footage.
