# Our Mission

Stockflow.media was built with one goal: to give creators and businesses access to **premium, high-resolution stock media** without the complexity or cost of traditional licensing.

---

## What We Stand For

We believe great visuals should be available to everyone — from an independent documentary filmmaker to a global hospitality brand. Our library is meticulously curated, professionally produced, and built to perform at the highest technical standards.

---

## What We Offer

| Media Type | Specs | Ideal For |
|---|---|---|
| **Video Footage (MP4)** | Up to 8K, 3 aspect ratios | Film, social media, presentations |
| **Photography (JPEG)** | Up to 8K resolution | Print, editorial, digital backgrounds |
| **Audio Assets (MP3)** | Professional grade audio | Soundscapes, music beds, ambient |
| **Vector Graphics** | SVG, EPS, AI formats | Branding, infographics, scalable design |

## Our Collections

Our library is organised into focused, purposeful collections:

- **Microscopic** — Virus particles, biological cells, fungi, bacteria, pollen grains, and more. Ideal for science, health, and technology storytelling.
- **Food & Beverage** — Beautifully plated dishes and ingredients, shot in multiple formats. Perfect for restaurants, food media, and lifestyle brands.

New categories are added regularly as we expand our production roster.
