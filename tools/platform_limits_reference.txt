================================================================
  PINTEREST SPECIFIC (Direct Bulk Upload)
================================================================

- Start at 5 pins/day (new account safety)
- Fresh pins ONLY — no repins
- Wait 7 days before pinning same link with new image
- Save to ONE board only per pin
- Consistency > Volume
- Increase to 10/day after 3 months
- Post times: 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00

================================================================
  RISK LEVELS
================================================================

LOW RISK:    1-3 posts/day on any platform
MEDIUM RISK: 4-8 posts/day (established accounts OK)
HIGH RISK:   9-15 posts/day (throttling likely)
DANGEROUS:   15+ posts/day (shadowban/suspension risk)

================================================================
  NOTES
================================================================

- YouTube API quota: 10,000 units/day, each upload ~1,600 units
  Max safe: 6 uploads/day (9,600 units)

- Instagram action blocks triggered by:
  Too many likes/follows/unfollows in short time
  Posting >5 times in quick succession
  Using banned hashtags

- LinkedIn penalizes:
  Multiple posts within 1 hour
  Posts with external links (lower reach)
  Company pages > personal profiles for limits

- Pinterest algorithm 2026:
  Rewards fresh unique content
  Penalizes multi-board saving
  Video pins get higher distribution

- Reddit:
  Karma gating on most subreddits
  10-min cooldown between posts (new accounts)
  Self-promo rules vary by subreddit
  Manual posting recommended — never automate
